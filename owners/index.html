Are you a hostel owner or manager around Nakawa, Port Bell Road, Kataza, or Kiswa? Connect directly with Victoria University students looking for accommodation and keep your rooms filled year-round. It's free, fast, and easy.

Why List Your Property?

🎯 Direct Student Reach

Target hundreds of Victoria University students and parents actively searching for housing near campus who otherwise rely purely on word-of-mouth.

🔄 Real-Time Availability Control

Instantly update your room statuses between "Free" and "Full" so students stop arriving when you are completely booked.

📞 Direct Inquiries

Receive direct phone calls and WhatsApp messages from prospective student tenants without paying middleman commission fees.

How the Listing Process Works

  1. Step 1: Create an Account
    Sign up using your name, phone number, email, and hostel name via our Owner Portal.
  2. Step 2: Submit Your Details
    Provide your room types, semester prices (UGX), amenities (water, electricity, Wi-Fi, security), and upload clear property photos.
  3. Step 3: Admin Review
    Our website administrator verifies every new listing for accuracy and security before it goes live.
  4. Step 4: Manage Your Rooms
    Log in anytime via Manage My Listing to update prices or mark room availability as things change.
