About Us

Welcome to the Victoria University Student Hostel Accommodation Finder. A platform built by students, for students, to solve the everyday challenge of finding reliable and verified accommodation near campus.

The Problem We Are Solving

Victoria University students who need a hostel around Nakawa cannot easily find out which hostels have free rooms, what they cost, and what amenities are included. Many students stay in private hostels around Nakawa, Port Bell Road, Kataza, and Kiswa. Because these hostels do not traditionally advertise, rooms are filled entirely by word of mouth, meaning students often discover a hostel is full only after arriving in person.
Furthermore, Victoria University's official contact page lists no private hostels.

Our Research & Evidence

We grounded our website in real-world data and investigation conducted in November 2026:

  • Phone Interviews: We phoned attendants at Laxy Courts, University Courts, and Biko hostels in Nakawa. All confirmed they do not advertise and rooms fill rapidly, with University Courts noting about a quarter of its tenants are from Victoria University.
  • Student Survey: In our Google Form survey, 6 out of 9 respondents reported visiting a hostel that was already full, and 8 out of 13 stated they would actively use a dedicated platform like ours.
  • Safety Standards: We reference Makerere University's 2023 hostel inspection report of 37 hostels to emphasize the importance of tracking security, CCTV, and fire safety compliance.
