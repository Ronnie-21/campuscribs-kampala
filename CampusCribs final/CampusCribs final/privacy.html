Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

Hostel owners who sign up or submit a listing also give us their name, phone number, email address and hostel details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are kept private and are only used by our administration team to respond to your inquiries, address feedback, or resolve technical errors. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

3. Hostel Bookings and Listings

Prices, room statuses (such as available or limited rooms), and hostel locations near campus are subject to change. Please coordinate directly with individual hostel management or our central office to confirm current rates and lease terms.

4. User Responsibilities

Users agree to provide accurate information when utilizing contact forms and interacting with the website directory. Misuse of the platform or submission of false error reports may result in restricted access.

5. Your Rights and Removal Requests

We handle personla data in line with Uganda's data Protection Act, 2019. You can ask to see details we hold about you ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them. To make a request, go to our Contact Us page, choose "Privacy Concern" as the message type, and which details you want removed. We reply within 24 hours on working days and will cinnfirm once your details are removed.
