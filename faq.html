Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from students and hostel owners.

What is CampusCribs Kampala?

It is a website that helps students find suitable accommodation near their universities at an affordable price.

Do I need an account to search for a hostel?

No, you can search, filter, and contact hostel owners without signing up. Only hostel owners need an account.

How can I find a hostel?

Go to "Find a Hostel", use the search filters to narrow down options based on your preferences, and then contact the hostel owner.

How do I know if a hostel has free rooms?

Hostel owners mark their listings as "available" or "limited rooms" to indicate current availability.

Is the room information always up to date?

Owners update it themselves and rooms can fill quickly. Always call or visit the hostel to confirm before you book.

What is usually included in the rent?

In our reviews, most hostels include Wi-Fi, running water, electricity, and security in their rent, but not meals.

Are the provided prices final?

No, prices can change, so it's best to confirm with the hostel owner before booking.

Can I trust the hostel's security details?

Security details are provided by the hostel owners. We do not inspect hostels, so we recommend visiting the property or contacting them directly to verify the security measures in place.

How can I list my hostel?

Go to "List Your Hostel", sign up, and then send your hostel details and photos. It is free to register.

Who approves new listings?

The website administrators review and approve all new hostel listings before they are published.

I found wrong information. What should I do?

Go to the Contact Us page and choose the "Report Incorrect Information" option so we can fix it.
